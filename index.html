04about

I'm a hobbyist developer. My interest in cybersecurity drove the passion to understand what programs actually do under the hood. That question led me deeper: debugging led to disassembly, disassembly led to PE headers and instructions, and instructions led to memory layouts, data and file formats, and eventually to the computational machinery and electrical currents beneath the interfaces we use everyday.

I learn by building. Sometimes that's a conventional application; more often it's a tool built to understand a technology or elimiate a repetitive task. Whenever I pick up a new language, my ritual is to implement DES by hand. Bit by bit, table by table. By the time a cipher encrypts correctly, I've mapped the language's rough edges.

I'm not a senior engineer and I don't larp as one on the internet. These projects are the working notes of a developing programmer, presented as honestly as I can - including what's unfinished.
